Privacy Policy

YOne Pro · Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how YOne Pro (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our casual multi-game hub on iOS. We designed the App to keep personal data collection to a minimum. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • You can start playing right away—no extra setup steps are required inside the App.
  • Game progress, settings, and similar data are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Information generated through use of the App

  • Gameplay and progress data — scores, level progress, difficulty preference, tutorial flags, and play statistics stored on device via the App’s local storage (e.g. UserDefaults).
  • Device context — minimal technical signals needed for gameplay and interface layout (for example, screen metrics). We do not use this to identify you personally.

Information we do not collect by default

  • Name, phone number, or postal address (unless you contact us voluntarily by email).
  • Precise location.
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone/camera content (the App does not require these permissions for core gameplay).

3. How We Use Information

We use locally stored data only to:

  • Run mini-games and remember your progress, bests, and settings;
  • Restore tutorials, difficulty, and hub preferences;
  • Keep the App stable and responsive during play.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law, legal process, or government request;
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the App;
  • With service providers who assist in operating the App under confidentiality obligations, and only to the extent necessary.

5. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App or use in-app reset options (where available). You may clear progress or tutorial flags from Settings within the App when those controls are offered.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is a casual game collection not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the data we handle. No storage method is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Reset scores, runs, or tutorials from in-app Settings where provided.
  • Delete the App to remove locally stored data from your device.

Depending on your region, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, objection). Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

9. International Users

If you use the App outside your home country, gameplay data is still processed and stored on your device unless you choose to contact us directly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.
